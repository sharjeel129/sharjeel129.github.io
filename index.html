Sharjeel Ahmad

Physics PhD · Yale

I am a Graduate Student in Physics at Yale University , where I am working with Prof. Shruti Puri on Quantum Error Benchmarking. My broad research interests include Quantum Information Science and Quantum Optics – in particular, I am interested in Quantum Error Correction and building fault-tolerant protocols using various analytical and computational tools.

I have previously worked with Prof. Paul Kwiat on multi-depth holography and linear optics as well as with Prof. Kejie Fang on Quantum Non-Gaussian Correlations, Photon Wavelength Conversion and Higher Dimensional Quantum Teleportation Protocols.

My non-academic interests include tennis, parliamentary debating and futures trading.
